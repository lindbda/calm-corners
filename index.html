For teachers

Share it with students

Use Copy link on any card and paste it into Clever, Google Classroom, or a bookmark. You can also link this page and let students choose. On a smartboard, press Full screen in the scene's toolbar.

Privacy

Nothing is collected or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking. Settings like a cat's name are saved only in that device's browser.

Good to know

  • Sound starts after the first tap. That's how browsers work.
  • Every note is from a five-note scale, so nothing ever clashes.
  • The breathing guide has three patterns: Balloon (4 in, 5 out), Square, and 4-7-8.
  • The screen stays awake during breathing and timers on most devices.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • B breathe · T timer · R rain · H music
  • M mute · F full screen · Esc close menus
  • Cozy Cat Room: C your cat · L lamp · P pet
  • Space drops a ripple or a ball of yarn
